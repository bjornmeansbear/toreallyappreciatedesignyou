One at random

In 2009, at a visiting artist workshop with Andrew Blauvelt, the prompt was to make a poster in response to Bernard Tschumi’s “To really appreciate architecture, you may even need to commit a murder” (Advertisements for Architecture, 1976–77). This was mine: a small Drupal site where anyone could finish the sentence for design. The site is gone. The endings are below, as visitors left them.
